# Changelog

All notable changes to Disciplined Times. The project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org); see `AGENTS.md` for when to bump which number.

## 0.5.1

- Contributor guidelines (`AGENTS.md`): AI agents now name branches `claude/<what-changed>` instead of auto-generated names. No changes to the app.

## 0.5.0

- **Keyboard shortcuts:** press `?` (or click **Keyboard shortcuts** at the bottom of the sidebar, or **Show shortcuts** in Settings on a computer) to see every shortcut. Pick one, or just press its keys, and a keyboard picture above the list lights up the keys to press. The keyboard matches your computer (Mac, Windows or Linux) and you can switch between them.
- Shortcuts now also work on non-English keyboard layouts (for example Russian, Greek or French AZERTY), and never fire together with Ctrl, Cmd, Alt/Option or AltGr.

## 0.4.0

- **Welcome tour:** on your first visit, a short walkthrough shows how to create a goal, log time (with a sample goal you can try, nothing is saved) and read the Week chart, and ends on your settings (theme and light/dark mode, installing as an app, which day the week starts, and how your data is stored). Changes you make there apply right away. It ends with buttons to create your first goal or open Settings with the install section in view.
- **Settings:** **Take the tour** shows the walkthrough again any time. People who already have goals or logged time won't see it on its own.

## 0.3.1

- **Week:** the chart now follows your finger while you swipe, and the new week slides in from the side (also when using the arrow buttons), with a little vibration on supported phones, so it's clear the week changed.

## 0.3.0

- **Settings:** a new **View changelog** button shows what changed in each version, right inside the app (works offline too).

## 0.2.0

- **Week:** swipe left or right on the weekly chart to go to the next or previous week.
- Added `AGENTS.md` with contributor and agent guidelines, including per-PR versioning, and this changelog.

## 0.1.3

- Install-as-app banner on mobile, and the top banners (install, updates) merged into one swipeable notification stack.
- Dropped the "Ready to work offline" notice.

## 0.1.2

- The app checks for updates when it comes back to the foreground and every hour.
- The source link opens in an external browser from the installed app.

## 0.1.1

- The source link opens in the default browser from the Android app.

## 0.1.0

- First release: goals planned per day, week or month; quick time logging; Week view; themes with light and dark modes; installable, offline-capable PWA.
